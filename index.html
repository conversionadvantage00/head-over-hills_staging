Dining

When you stay with us at Head over Hills, you don’t have to leave the hotel for a first-class lunch or dinner. You might not want to miss the stunning sunset, or you may feel like a night in.

In season we serve lunch and dinner daily. Our private chef composes a daily à la carte menu where you can choose to have one, two or three courses. So, stay at Head over Hills for lunch and dinner and enjoy the best view in Knysna.

View our Menu

Wine Cellar

At Head over Hills, we are passionate about fine wine. Martin the owner, has created a wine cellar where the guests can walk in and pick their very own special bottle to enjoy while watching the sunset or with one of our delicious à la carte dinners.

South African vineyards produce some of the best wines in the world. In our wine cellar, we mainly concentrate on South African wines. Some of the finest vineyards are represented, including Hartenberg, Kaapzicht, Cederberg and Simonsig.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer wedding packages at Head over Hills?

We offer tailored wedding experiences to suit your vision. Enquire with us to create your dream wedding.

How many guests can Head over Hills accommodate for a wedding?

Our villa is perfect for intimate weddings. Contact us for details on guest capacity and accommodation options.

Can we book the entire villa for a private event?

Yes, Head over Hills can be exclusively booked for weddings, celebrations, or corporate events.

What activities are available nearby?

From golfing and hiking to sailing and wine tasting, there's plenty to explore in and around Knysna.

Do you offer spa treatments?

Yes, we offer massage treatments on our balcony, allowing you to relax while taking in the breathtaking ocean views. Speak to our staff to arrange a session during your stay.

Is there parking at the hotel?

Yes, we offer secure parking for our guests.

Do you cater to dietary restrictions at the restaurant?

Absolutely! Let us know in advance, and our private chef will gladly accommodate your dietary needs.

What is the best way to get to Head over Hills?

We are approximately a 40-minute drive from George Airport. Private transfers can be arranged upon request.
